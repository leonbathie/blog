West African initiatory tales, such as the fables of *Bouki the hyena and Leuk the hare* or classic stories like *Majnun and Layla*, are complex systems of moral and linguistic transmission. Making them accessible to the new generation on **Taro Learning** means solving a production challenge: generating consistent 3D animation at a low marginal cost.

### Generative pipeline & dedicated Chrome extension

We designed a toolchain that combines natural synthetic voices in Pulaar and Wolof with generative video workflows (**Google Flow / Veo 3**). A custom Chrome extension orchestrates it: it automates the timing cuts, keeps the characters consistent (*Character Seed Locking*) and assembles the final sequences.
